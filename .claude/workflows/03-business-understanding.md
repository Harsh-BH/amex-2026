# Workflow 03 — Business Understanding

**Objective:** A shared, written model of Premier-Card issuer economics that the framework will encode.
**Prerequisites:** Workflow 01.

## Steps
1. Invoke **business-analysis**; review `business-context.md` and the product overview.
2. Enumerate revenue levers and cost levers; map each to feature(s).
3. Define "good" vs "bad" cardmember in P&L terms.
4. Note where ground truth likely comes from (revenue−cost on these features) → informs design.

## Validation
- Every feature has a tentative revenue/cost/risk role with a mechanism.

## Deliverables
- Updated `business-context.md` + `feature-notes.md` roles.

## Exit criteria
- The economic spine is written down and agreed.
