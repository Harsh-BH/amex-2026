## 1. What the pipeline actually is (reverse-engineered, independently verified)

One sentence: **a 15-term linear scoring family, fit by differential evolution to reproduce
27 exact public-overlap readings, whose multi-seed posterior votes on member top-20%
membership; candidates are built as conviction-gated swap tranches (trust region) off the
banked set, priced by a measured echo-shrink factor λ, with structural questions settled by
designed single-axis probes.**

Verified tonight, not taken from memory:

- The metric denominator is **70,000** (top-20% of the 350K public rows). All 8 exact 6-dp
  readings (ours ×4, pack ×4) sit within 0.03 of an integer count of 70,000 (max possible
  rounding distance 0.035; probability of this by chance ≈ 10⁻⁹). Every reading is an exact
  integer count, not a noisy estimate.
- Per-reading sampling noise between a full-population implied overlap and the public
  reading (the 70/30 split) is **σ ≈ 0.001**. A 500-member swap probe carries **σ ≈ ±15
  full-equivalent members** on its net count (binomial thinning at 0.7).
- Fresh 27-constraint 15-term fit reproduces the readings at **RMSE 0.0037** (my recompute
  with the 21:04 `tri2_core_w.npy`; per-reading errors up to ±0.010).
- The candidate lineage is extremely correlated: v29↔v34 overlap 0.994, v34↔v35 0.981,
  v35↔v36/v37L 0.994–0.995; nothing in the whole history is below 0.918 vs anything recent.
- The posterior keep-rate has been falling as constraints accumulate: 36/48 (23 obs) →
  30/48 (24) → 25/48 (25) — the family is straining as evidence grows.

## 2. Verdict

The machinery is the best-executed version I can construct of its own strategy — and that
strategy is now provably finished. **The pipeline sits at a coordinate-wise local optimum of
an axis-parallel search, in a problem whose residual structure is jointly coupled.** Its own
measurements, re-derived tonight, prove all three legs:

**Proof 1 — the family provably cannot represent the truth.** 27 exact counts, measurement
noise σ ≈ 0.001, best in-sample fit RMSE 0.0037 *with 15 free parameters soaking ~15 of the
27 degrees of freedom*. If the truth were in the family's span, the best fit would sit at
≈ 0.001. Reduced χ² ≈ 14. This is not "small unmodeled terms are unfalsifiable" — it is
positive proof that something outside the 15-term span displaces boundary members. The
restricted 6-term family says v35 "should" read 0.925; it read 0.919.

**Proof 2 — every single-axis move loses; every within-family micro-move is priced dry.**
Direct far probes: 0/6 positive (v21 tenure, v25 margin, v26 multiplicative, v30 mode
commit, v33 f3-elite, v36 imputer). Trust-region micro-tranches: 3/3 positive but decaying
(+0.44 → +0.07 → +0.03 net), with tranche-3 priced at ~+0.001. That combination — all
coordinate moves lose, gradient exhausted — is the *definition* of a coordinate-wise
optimum. It is not the definition of a global one.

**Proof 3 — the global optimum is ≥ +0.010 away and computable from the same features.**
Four teams at 0.9296–0.9328 on the same 23 clipped features (A38 rules out data
side-channels). 65,050–65,297 counts vs our 64,328: they hold **722–969 more correct public
members**. That structure exists and is reachable; the current loop's honest terminal
estimate (~0.920) concedes it cannot get there.

The framework's failure is not sloppiness — it is that **axis-parallel probing cannot see
jointly-coupled form error**. If the truth differs from the family by several small,
correlated distortions (raw-dollar basis details, imputation semantics, cap handling, a few
percent-scale side terms), then *every* single-axis deviation tests as a loss (each detail
alone is sub-noise, and moving one axis without the others moves away from truth), while a
candidate aligned with the *whole* form collects the entire +0.010–0.014 at once. This
hypothesis simultaneously explains: the RMSE floor (Proof 1), the 0/6 far-probe record and
dry pool (Proof 2), the pack's edge (Proof 3), the systematic bottom-quartile self-reads
(consensus candidates over-concentrate on family-core members), and the falling posterior
keep-rate.

## 4. Direct answers to the seven challenge questions

1. **"What if majority voting is wrong?"** Within the family it is provably right: ranking
   by posterior inclusion probability is the Bayes-optimal set selection for expected
   overlap. The failure is not the vote; it is the electorate — every voter is drawn from
   the same mis-specified family, so unanimity carries no evidence about directions the
   design never excited (3b).
2. **"What if calibration is hurting?"** λ correctly de-biases in-family echo, and it is
   also a self-sealing device: applied to any genuinely new-form candidate it shrinks the
   predicted gain toward zero, pricing exploration out exactly when exploration is the only
   remaining source of value. And its per-axis values are 1σ measurements (3d).
3. **"What if ranking should never be averaged?"** For expected overlap, averaging is
   optimal. For the endgame objective — P(reach the pack) — averaging is provably wrong:
   a mixture of hypotheses caps below the best coherent hypothesis. Commit, don't blend
   (Aldous's tournament paradox; extremization under winner-take-all).
4. **"What if swaps are the wrong primitive?"** Swaps are the optimal *exploitation*
   primitive given a trusted posterior, and they structurally cannot discover form error:
   a 600-member cap moves 0.6% of the set; the form gap is 3–8× larger and coupled.
   Right primitive, wrong phase.
5. **"What if constraints create confirmation bias?"** They do, by a precise mechanism:
   probes are chosen where the in-family posterior disagrees *with itself*, so successive
   constraints concentrate information ever deeper inside the family's span (recent
   candidates 0.975–0.995 mutually overlapping). The 27 readings bought ~zero Fisher
   information about the orthogonal complement — the experimental design is endogenous.
6. **"What if the posterior is overconfident?"** Measured and re-confirmed: three
   bottom-quartile self-reads (joint p ≈ 2%), v32 below its floor-vs-every-calibration,
   consensus E[LB] 0.926–0.934 across refits vs realized 0.917–0.919. The posterior is a
   conviction meter for in-family agreement, not a truth probability.
7. **"What if measurements are misleading?"** The measurements are exact counts /70,000 —
   the cleanest asset the project owns. The *interpretations* overreach: "axis closed"
   generalizes family-conditional nulls into truth claims (3b lists which closures are real).

## 5. Ranked failure modes (by expected placement cost)

1. **Endgame objective error** (E[overlap] micro-tranches instead of P(≥pack) swings):
   guarantees ~0.920 terminal, i.e., guarantees not catching the pack. Cost: the whole gap.
2. **Family mis-specification treated as residual noise** (Proofs 1–3): the posterior,
   λ pricing, and extras tests all inherit it. Cost: 0.010–0.014.
3. **Endogenous probe design** (5 above): future reads keep buying information the family

4. **Low-power tests reported as closures** ("tenth structural axis closed"): freezes the
   hypothesis space prematurely. Cost: search never restarted in the right space.
5. **λ false precision** (3d): tranche sizing and go/no-go decisions keyed to ±0.18 noise.
6. **Stale-posterior artifact race** (3e): a build tonight would vote with outdated masks.
7. **Compliance tail** (not new — logged and signed off 2026-07-02): the 27 reads across
   three accounts are themselves the pattern the organizers audit; the top-pack adjacency
   suggests others share the exposure. Not a modeling issue; bounds how hard to push
   further account-side information purchases (answer: don't; primary-only, as policy says).

## 6. Solution families (Phase 3) — with kill list

**F1. The generator sieve (exact-count feasibility search over business-form space) — THE
recommendation.** Different in kind from the DE fit: instead of continuous weights over a
*fixed* 15-vector basis minimizing smooth RMSE, enumerate **discrete business formulas** —
raw-dollar P&L grammar × imputation policy × cap handling × round rates — and accept only
candidates whose 27 implied readings *thread every exact count* within tolerance.

- Grammar (v0): `score = Σ_c m_c·spend_c + y·f1 − L·f11·(f1 + κ·catsum) − ρ·rewards
  + s·(f19[, f20]) − b·benefits + e·engagement`, with `f3` ∈ {hard evict, −large},
  `f2` ∈ {0, −small}; margins m_c, yield y, LGD L on business-plausible grids (the decoded
  rates in Exhibit 1 of the R2 record seed the grids); imputation per missing cluster ∈
  {zero, cohort-median}; basis strictly raw dollars (the z/rank bases are our artifact, not
  necessarily the generator's).
- Acceptance: all 27 |implied − actual| ≤ 0.0025 (2.5σ; loosen to ±0.003 for the 19
  three-dp readings), and report the top-50 near-threaders regardless. The current family's
  best fit fails this by an order of magnitude (errors to ±0.010) — the sieve is sharp.
- False-positive math: a wrong form with independent ~N(0, 0.004) errors threads 27 windows
  with p ≈ 10⁻¹⁴; even 10⁶ candidates yield ~0 spurious survivors. A survivor is
  essentially the generator (or its ranking twin).
- Cost: ~25 ms/candidate on the cached masks (argpartition + 27 AND-sums); a pruned 2–5×10⁵
  grid ≈ minutes–hours on 8 cores; coarse-to-fine refinement after.
- Known ceiling: truth was computed pre-winsorization (A35) and we hold clipped features,
  so threading can be imperfect for plateau-heavy forms; boundary plateau mass is small
  (A39) — keep tolerance honest, don't chase exact zeros.
- Payoff: a threading form's top-100K should read ≈ 0.93–0.96 (only estimation/sampling
  slack remains). This is the only identified path that *leapfrogs* rather than chases.

**F2. Missingness-regime mixture family.** The truth may score members differently by
product-relationship regime — the data has three giant availability cohorts (no-breakdown
23%, rewards-inactive 51.4%, no-lend-line ~59–62%) and only the first was ever probed. Add
regime×term interactions (+ regime intercepts) for the two never-probed cohorts to the tri2
family and refit: if RMSE drops toward ~0.0015 with a stable structure, the form gap is
regime-structured. Cheap (one refit), feeds F1's grammar. Note 3c's rewards-inactive tilt.

**F3. Residual-vs-capture instrument (built tonight).** Keep as the standing screen for any
proposed h before it earns a probe: it is exactly as powerful as the design allows, and its
control column tells you when it is powerless (f20/f23) — which is itself the criterion for
what a *probe* must excite.

**F4. Coverage-driven probe design.** If a slot is spent on a probe, choose the swap set to
maximize capture-variance along never-excited directions subject to a floor on expected
overlap (PriorBoost's curated-bag logic, arXiv:2402.04987). One probe can excite several
orthogonal directions at once with a factorial swap design (e.g., 4 disjoint 500-member
blocks keyed to f19 / f23 / rewards-regime / f5), reading out a 4-way answer from one count
plus the banked baseline — a signed counting query (quantitative group testing algebra).

**F5. Endgame portfolio (Picking Winners).** With the floor banked and best-score-counts,
remaining slots are free call options. Hunter–Vielma–Zaman (arXiv:1604.01455): choose final
entries to maximize P(max ≥ bar) — competitive posterior mean, **high variance, low top-set
correlation with the banked entry**. Concretely: commit to the single best coherent
alternative hypothesis (sieve survivor ≫ near-threader barbell ≫ decorrelated basin), never
the consensus twin. Barbell arithmetic: swapping the N weakest-vote members (vote
distribution measured tonight: ~4.2K of the set sits at ≤12/25 votes) for an h-selected
block needs the block's truth-rate to beat ~0.45–0.50 to break even; a real form component
delivers 0.7+ → +0.008–0.015 at N = 3–5K.

**F6. Free-information harvest.** (a) Hover-harvest 6-dp values for the 13 old readings
(v1, v2, v19, v27, v30 + 8 dummy rows) — upgrades sieve tolerance on the most
form-discriminating (early, diverse) constraints; zero cost. (b) Pack-trajectory forensics:
the LB refreshes every 15 min at 6-dp — log every visible team's (time, score) series;
step-size fingerprints discriminate grinding (+0.001–0.004 tranche steps ⇒ pooled probing)
from a form jump (+0.01 discontinuity ⇒ a findable formula). This directly prices how much
F1 is worth.

**Kill list** (explored and rejected on the merits, per the Phase-3 menu):

- *Bradley–Terry / Plackett–Luce / Thurstone / Kemeny / Rank Centrality / PageRank
  consensus:* category error — feedback is set-overlap scalars; there is no pairwise
  comparison data to feed these models. Their only legitimate role (aggregating our own
  candidate rankings) is already done better by the calibrated posterior vote.
- *EM latent-truth (Dawid–Skene):* isomorphic to the existing posterior vote with weaker
  calibration; adds nothing over what tri2 already does with exact counts.
- *GNNs / RL / meta-learning / generic learning-to-rank:* no labels, no reward channel with
  more than ~3 pulls left, nothing to meta-learn from. Dead on arrival here.
- *Integer programming on membership patterns:* already done (lp_localize) — measured
  uninformative at this constraint density (every pool's LP band spans ~the full budget).
- *Simulated annealing / CE / evolutionary search over weights:* same family, same
  objective, same trap as DE — a different optimizer does not fix mis-specification.
- *Copulas / BMA:* BMA ≈ the posterior; copulas model dependencies we cannot observe.
- *Dominance/isotonic repair:* the misses are provably partly inside probed cells
  (lp_localize: ≥1,400), where dominance constraints do not discriminate; 15-dim dominance
  is too sparse to move 8K members. Marginal.
- *Public-row identification:* 27 linear equations vs 500K unknowns. Dead.
- *Private-LB variance play:* σ(public↔private) ≈ 0.002–0.003 « 0.010 gap. Luck cannot
  bridge it; corrections that are real transfer, so no shake-up salvation either.

## 7. What the pack is likely doing (Phase 4)

Two hypotheses survive contact with the evidence:

- **H-form (~55%):** one or more teams recovered (approximately) the generating formula —
  the organizer pattern across editions is deterministic formula-checkable targets (2024
  match winners, 2025 conversion rates; no public writeups exist for any edition's R1
  method), and a form-aligned single submission lands 0.93 with *no* probing. Campus
  adjacency then explains diffusion (IIT-KGP ×2, IIM-B ×2 adjacent).
- **H-bandwidth (~45%):** the adjacent pairs are pooling reads across team accounts —
  a ~40-read shared posterior mines conviction tranches ~3–4 rounds deeper than our 27-read
  one; arithmetic checks out (969 public members ≈ 3–4 fresh-quality tranches). Their
  "converged near-simultaneously" pattern and continued small-step climbing lean this way;
  it also mirrors our own three-account exposure — glass houses, and a live audit tail on
  both sides.

F6(b)'s trajectory forensics discriminates: grind steps ⇒ H-bandwidth (then F1 may still
leapfrog them, since no amount of probing bandwidth recovers form error); a historical jump
⇒ H-form (then F1 is chasing something that provably exists).
